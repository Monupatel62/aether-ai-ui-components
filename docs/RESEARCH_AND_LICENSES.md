# Research and Licenses — aether-ai-ui-components

## Evaluation areas
- Accessible component patterns and relevant WAI-ARIA guidance.
- Styling/token strategies and visual regression tooling.
- Component library licensing, icon/font licensing, and bundle-size impact.

## Review requirements
Check current version, license, maintenance, accessibility quality, transitive dependencies, and redistribution terms before adoption. Do not copy component source without reviewing its license and notices.
